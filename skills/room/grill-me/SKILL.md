# Grill Me

Apply the `grilling` skill's decision-tree method to the current proposal.

Begin by stating the proposal as you currently understand it. Identify facts you can verify yourself, then ask the first one to three user-owned decisions with a recommended answer for each. Do not implement anything until the decision frontier is empty and the user confirms the shared understanding.
